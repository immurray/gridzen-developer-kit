# From installation to first use

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install gridzen-developer-kit
.venv/bin/gridzen start --task identity_onboarding --lang en
```

1. Run the anonymous synthetic trial for success, rejection and unavailable outcomes.
2. Register and verify your email only when you need persistent sandbox records.
3. In Console, run a new sandbox request. Identity sessions require the available provider sandbox; the phone adapter is synthetic.
4. Optionally consent to account/project progress measurement, then complete a new request on a different UTC day. Polling an old record is not reuse.
5. The Console TEST wallet demonstrates orders, debit, refund and reconciliation with simulated credits. It never charges money or enables production.

Source categories are user-confirmed, not verified acquisition attribution. Linked measurement expires after 90 days and can be withdrawn/deleted without losing service. A separate reminder subscription is available only after the independent customer mailbox passes acceptance; at most two emails, at least 72 hours apart. Reply, activation or unsubscribe stops it.

Do not put customer keys, real documents or conversations into marketing prompts. The five research/synthetic MCP tools and 14 Skills (6 research/workflow Skills and 8 single-task profiles, including 3 offline and 5 merchant profiles requiring separate authorization) retain their existing boundaries. Production access, provider rights and prices require a separate review.


## What the fourteen Skills enable

Of the fourteen bundled Skills, nine provide research, synthetic integration,
local utilities or bounded draft workflows. They do not require a paid merchant verification purchase. Five
merchant profiles use an enrolled merchant's existing API key, live capability
status, actual service credit and per-task authorization. Installing all fourteen
Skills does not grant fourteen production services.

A bank-name `review` result concerns that task's evidence; it does not suspend the
merchant or other Skills. Read its reason and original record instead of changing
the name and paying again. Grade B means a supported comparison of sourced names;
it is not a failed task, document identity, direct bank-CURP binding or current
account control. The calling business decides whether that evidence meets its
use case. Neither result should be rewritten as unconditional approval.

The hosted journey is completed by the person on the provider page. Read the
original task for its authoritative result. A live authorized hosted journey has
been accepted with signed notifications and authoritative completion; this
single-case test does not enable customer purchases. Current capability and
balance responses remain authoritative. An internal company-funded acceptance
credit is not a customer payment or general merchant credit.
